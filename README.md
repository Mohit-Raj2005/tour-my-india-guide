# Tour My India Guide 🌏🇮🇳
>
> ⚠️ **Note:** This repository is private and not intended for cloning or public use.  
> The content here is for reference and project documentation only.
> 
**Tour My India Guide** is a complete travel and tour guide platform designed to make exploring India and the world easier, richer, and more engaging. From weekend getaways to international adventures, it brings everything under one roof.

## ✨ Features

- **Destinations**: Discover popular spots and hidden gems across India.  
- **Wedding Plans**: Curated destination wedding packages.  
- **Holiday Packages**: Family trips, romantic escapes, adventure tours.  
- **International Tours**: Explore multiple countries with tailored packages.  
- **Wildlife Safaris**: Experience India’s biodiversity with guided safaris.  
- **Weekend Plans**: Quick escapes and short trip suggestions.  
- **Beach Packages**: Relaxing coastal holidays with curated stays.  
- **Hotel Bookings**: Easy booking options for hotels and resorts.  
- **Virtual Tours**: Heritage sites of India brought to life online.  
- **Custom Packages**: Personalized itineraries for unique travel needs.  
- **And More**: Continuous expansion with new features and destinations.
